Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Afterword holds words that are deeply personal. This page explains, in plain language, what we keep, why, and how you can remove it.

What we keep

  • Your email address and password, so you can sign in. Your password is stored in a protected form, and we can’t read it.
  • The letters you write, and who each one is for.
  • The name and email address of the trusted person you choose.
  • The date of your latest “I’m still here” check-in.

Why we keep it

Only to do what Afterword promises: to keep your letters safe, to notice when you haven’t checked in, and to deliver your letters to the people you chose once your trusted person confirms. We don’t use your letters for anything else, and we don’t read them.

Who handles it

Your information is stored with Supabase, in a data centre in Europe. Emails are sent through Resend. These services process your information only to run Afterword for you. We never sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your trusted person

When you add a trusted person, we will email them to tell them they have been named, and later, if it comes to it, to ask them to confirm. Please make sure they are happy to be your trusted person before you add them.

Removing your information

You can change or delete any letter at any time. You can also ask us to delete your account, which removes your letters, your trusted person and your check-in history. Once deleted, it can’t be brought back.

Questions

If you have a question about your information, write to us at mwauraelvis54@gmail.com.
