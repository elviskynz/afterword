Afterword

Say what you want them to know, while you can.

How Afterword works

It is a quiet place to write down what matters, so the people you love hear it from you.

The steps

  1. Write your letters. One for each person, in your own words.
  2. Choose a trusted person. Someone who will tell Afterword when the time has come.
  3. Your letters stay private. Nobody reads them, and nothing is sent, while you are here.
  4. Your trusted person confirms. Each letter is then emailed to the person it was written for.

What you will need

  • An email address for each person you are writing to.
  • One trusted person who has agreed to do this, and their email address.
  • An Afterword account, so your letters are saved safely.
  • A little quiet time. You do not have to finish in one sitting.

Sign in to begin

Create an account the first time. Use a password of at least 8 characters.

Your letters

Write one letter for each person. Take your time. You can come back and change them whenever you like.

Your monthly check-in

Once a month, let Afterword know you are well. If you stop checking in, your trusted person is asked to step in. Nothing is sent without you being given the chance to stop it.

Your trusted person

Choose someone who will let Afterword know when your letters should be sent.

Delete your account

This removes your letters, your trusted person and your check-in history for good. It cannot be undone.

Sending is not switched on yet. Your letters are saved privately to your account, and you can download a copy of each one. Emailing them after a trusted person confirms is the next thing to be set up.
